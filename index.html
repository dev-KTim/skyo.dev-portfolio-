flagship, in progress, private repo

              
ki-videos

              
A pipeline that turns a script into a finished 9:16 video: Remotion and Three.js render 3D scenes, Gemini speaks the voiceover and returns word timings, a deterministic effects library adds 24 visual effects and a sound database, and a QA step checks loudness, black frames and format before Playwright posts it to TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

              
    
                
  • TypeScript
  • Remotion
  • Three.js
  • Python
  • Playwright
    • 
              

              Private for now. Ask me for a walkthrough.
